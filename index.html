User guide

Make a 4×6 nine-up print

This page lays out nine photos on one portrait 4×6 inch sheet. The frames stay locked in a 3×3 grid with no gaps. You only move and resize the picture inside each frame.

1. Add photos

  • Click an empty slot to choose one image.
  • Drop up to nine files onto the print at once.

2. Move a photo

  • Click the photo you want to edit.
  • Drag in any direction — left, right, up, or down.
  • The frame stays put; only the image inside it moves.

3. Resize a photo

  • Click a photo so crop brackets appear on its corners.
  • Pull a corner to zoom that patch in or out.
  • Scroll the mouse wheel over the photo to resize it.
  • Use − / + when a photo is selected.

4. Replace or remove

  • Replace swaps the selected photo.
  • Clear or Delete empties that slot.
  • Double-click a photo to reset its crop.

5. Save the print

  • Download PNG saves a 4×6 file at 300 DPI (1200 × 1800 px) for a photo lab.
  • Print sends the sheet to your printer at 4×6 inches.
